# Apple Health: HRV export

- [CODE] Apple Health's `heartRateVariabilitySDNN` is the standard deviation of normal RR intervals, expressed in time units (typically milliseconds): https://developer.apple.com/documentation/healthkit/hkquantitytypeidentifier/heartratevariabilitysdnn. Freeband's nightly RMSSD is a different statistic (`ios/Freeband/Analytics/HRV.swift`); it cannot be converted to SDNN from that aggregate alone.
- [CODE] Sleep files provide RR intervals in 10 ms increments; their packet timestamps have unresolved units (`docs/ACTIVITY.md` §6.3.2, `ios/Freeband/Analytics/HRV.swift`).
- [UNVERIFIED] Export SDNN computed directly from RR intervals in individually timestamped, sufficiently clean five-minute windows. Exclude intervals not participating in a valid adjacent pair (300–2000 ms, at most 20% jump); calculate the population standard deviation of the remaining intervals and round to milliseconds. These filters cannot establish that every beat is a normal sinus beat, and 10 ms resolution makes the result approximate. Skip windows outside the sleep session, with ambiguous timestamps, or insufficient data. Freeband's own RMSSD remains unchanged.
- [UNVERIFIED] On device: enable the new HRV write permission, sync a night with RR data, and check the Health app's HRV samples and their times against the sleep session. Compare with an independent reference device; no equivalence to Apple Watch measurements is assumed.
